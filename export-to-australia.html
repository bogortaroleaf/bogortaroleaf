Our Product

Dried Sliced Beneng Taro Leaves

Premium Grade A — Export Quality

Our dried Beneng taro leaves (Xanthosoma undipes) are harvested at peak maturity from Bogor's local farms, carefully sliced, and naturally sun-dried to preserve colour, texture, and nutritional value.

Beneng taro is a variety native to West Java, rich in dietary fibre, protein, vitamins, and minerals — increasingly sought after in international markets for its versatility in food, herbal, and wellness applications.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Australian Buyers

Can you ship dried taro leaves to Australia?
Yes. We ship directly from Bogor, Indonesia to Australia. We have experience preparing export documentation and can provide phytosanitary certificates and other paperwork required for Australian customs. Please contact us to discuss your specific order and location.
Are your taro leaves compliant with Australian biosecurity regulations?
Our dried taro leaves are naturally processed without chemical additives and are prepared with export compliance in mind. Dried plant products may be subject to inspection by DAFF (Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry). We can provide a phytosanitary certificate and full documentation to support customs clearance. We recommend checking the DAFF Biosecurity Import Conditions (BICON) database for the latest requirements.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for Australia?
